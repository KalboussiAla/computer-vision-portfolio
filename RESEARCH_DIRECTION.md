# Research Direction

My strongest direction is **applied visual intelligence**: computer vision systems that combine robust models, high-quality datasets, reliable evaluation, efficient deployment, and real operational value.

## Core Research Axis

**Context-aware and efficient computer vision for real-world systems**

This axis connects my applied work:

- industrial object detection and counting;
- fine-grained recognition;
- anomaly detection;
- visual retrieval;
- annotation systems;
- active learning and semi-annotation;
- process optimization;
- deployment and operational reporting.

## Why This Direction

In real projects, the hard part is rarely "train a model once." The hard part is:

- choosing what to annotate next;
- detecting label mistakes;
- handling domain shift;
- measuring the right metric;
- reducing false positives;
- turning model outputs into decisions;
- making models efficient enough to run in real systems;
- keeping the system reliable on imperfect images and videos.

## Research Questions I Want to Explore

1. How can neuroscience-inspired ideas help make AI models more energy-efficient?
2. How can attention, sparse computation, memory, or continual learning reduce the gap between biological intelligence and current deep learning systems?
3. How can compact language or reasoning modules add context to vision models?
4. Can a small context-aware module reduce false positives in industrial or safety-critical vision systems?
5. How can computer vision detect rare events, anomalies, road risks, disasters, or unsafe behavior with limited examples?
6. How can active learning reduce annotation cost in industrial object detection?
7. How can foundation model embeddings improve fine-grained recognition under domain shift?
8. Could segmentation of a few examples, followed by vision-language retrieval, help discover similar examples for dataset expansion?
9. How can AI agents automate dataset analysis, experiment review, deployment reporting, and decision support?
10. How can applied AI reduce energy waste, production delays, material loss, and environmental cost?

## Long-Term Goal

My long-term goal is to build research-driven AI systems that become real products: tools that help factories, retailers, labs, smart infrastructure, and climate-aware operations use visual intelligence reliably.

I am especially interested in systems where AI is not only accurate, but also efficient, contextual, explainable, and useful in real workflows.
